## Goal

Research compounds instead of being one-off: a watchlist, scheduled re-runs, and a clear view of
what changed since the last report.

## Scope

- A watchlist with statuses (researching / watching / holding / rejected), notes, and where each
  idea came from.
- Scheduled re-research (e.g. quick daily, full weekly), configurable per token.
- Report diffs: holder concentration, liquidity, new or resolved red flags.
- Freshness badges, and a "What am I considering?" view.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Adding a token to the watchlist schedules its re-runs.
- [ ] Diffs highlight material changes between report versions.
- [ ] Stale research is visibly flagged.
