## Goal

Close the feedback loop from the [vision](../VISION.md#feedback-loop): narratives and theses with
history, decisions recorded, trades linked back to the thesis behind them, and reviews that separate
decision quality from outcome.

## Scope

- **Narratives** (e.g. RWA, Robinhood Chain): the thesis, evidence for and against, catalysts,
  linked tokens, and stage. Versioned, so changes in belief stay visible.
- **Theses** per candidate or position, with explicit invalidation conditions. AI-assisted drafting
  from research reports covers the vision's thesis-creation tooling.
- **Decisions** (pass / watch / buy, with reasoning) and scheduled **reviews**.
- **Automatic linking:** a detected trade on a token with an open thesis is linked to it.
- Exposure by narrative in the portfolio views.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A trade on a token with an open thesis is linked automatically.
- [ ] Invalidation conditions are visible and checkable.
- [ ] A narrative's full history of changes can be viewed.
